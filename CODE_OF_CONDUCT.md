# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want contributing to this list to be a good experience for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background or affiliation.

## Our standards

Expected:

- discuss the work, not the person;
- assume that a mislabelled entry is a mistake, not an agenda;
- give specific, actionable review feedback;
- accept that the maintainers make the final call on scope and taxonomy.

Not acceptable:

- personal attacks, harassment, or sustained disruption;
- publishing anyone's private information;
- promotional pull requests whose purpose is visibility rather than coverage.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and any other project space.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening an issue with the `conduct` label, or by contacting
a maintainer privately through their GitHub profile. Maintainers will respond as
quickly as they can and may edit, hide or reject contributions, or block
accounts, when this document is violated.

This document is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), simplified for a
curation project.
